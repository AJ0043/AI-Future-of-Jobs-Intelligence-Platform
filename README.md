## 🧠 About The Project

<table>
<tr>
<td>

### 🌍 Understanding the Future of Work

**AI Future of Jobs – Intelligence Platform** is a Data Analytics project designed to understand the changing global job market.

The project analyzes **30,000 job records** across multiple industries and locations to study:

* 💼 Job demand
* 💰 Salary trends
* 🤖 AI impact
* ⚙️ Automation risk
* 🌍 Remote work
* 🎓 Education requirements
* 📈 Experience requirements
* 🔮 Future job demand
* 👥 Gender diversity

</td>
</tr>
</table>

## 📋 Dataset Features

| -------------------------- | --------------------------------- |

## ❓ Business Questions

### 💼 Job Market

* Which industries have the highest job demand?
* Which job roles have the highest current openings?
* Which roles are projected to have higher demand in 2030?
* How does job status vary across industries?

### 💰 Salary Intelligence

* Which job roles have higher median salaries?
* How does salary change with experience?
* How does salary vary across industries?
* Does education requirement relate to salary?

### 🤖 AI & Automation

* How does AI impact vary across industries?
* What relationship exists between AI Impact Level and Automation Risk?
* Which industries have higher automation risk?
* Is automation risk associated with decreasing job status?

### 🌍 Future of Work

* Which roles show the largest change between 2024 and 2030?
* Which industries show stronger projected job demand?
* How does remote work vary across industries and locations?
* How does gender diversity vary across the job market?
